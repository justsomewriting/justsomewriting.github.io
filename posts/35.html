最美好的前途 - 佚名合唱

Stars

Deeply depressed
Highly pressed, by family and else
Moderately stressed
Somewhat drunk
No bright future ahead

Looks up at the sky

stars

billions of years of evolution

stars

millions of years of travelling light

stars

thousands of years of war and conquest

stars
stars

Hello, they say, how have you been?
Hello, they say, I hope I can see you again.

stars
Inter-mingling, dancing, blinking,
In a show of radiance,
Spilling their brightness onto us,
Preparing, singing
In a chorus,
Plucking frequencies that combine into
Positrons, electrons
Quarks, mesons
Photons, gravitons,
Light!

And so they say,
Let there be light!
Let the seas follow the marked boundaries,
Let the peacemakers be blessed...
And don't look back,
Lest you be turned
Into a pillar
Of salt
Of gluons and neutrinos and exotic matter.

Goodbye, they say, we come from the past
And there is nothing here anymore
Nothing important. Nothing bright
As the light
leaves us at the speed of time.

Hello, I say.
Quite a bit delayed, unheard by anyone,
As the tardy photons reach my retina,
Thus separated, forever,
beyond my power,
From

stars,
stars.
stars.



-2026.4.21, Waterloo ON, Tony Su
